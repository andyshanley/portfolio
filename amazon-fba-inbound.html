Amazon Seller · FBA Inbound · 2021

FBA Inbound Research & North Star

In 2020-21, I redesigned the entire end-to-end Amazon Seller inbound shipments CX

At a glance

The challenge

Through reviewing Seller feedback (through Amazon’s internal HMD/How’s My Driving platform) it was found that the end-to-end Seller inbound journey suffered from a severe lack of interoperability, as the UX was incoherently spread across multiple areas of Amazon’s Seller Central platform. This was leading to an increase in time on task and number of/repetitive steps to task completion for Amazon’s Selling Partners.

The objective

Partner with/encourage FBA Inbound product and tech owners to begin adopting a multi-year, vastly-improved Seller-centric “north star” CX into their respective roadmaps starting 2021.

The solution

Run an in-depth Seller research study, create an end-to-end Customer journey map, an accompanying data-centric research report, and new “north star” UI designs.

The result

Multiple Seller-centric initiatives were added to Amazon’s Operational Planning process and prioritized on multiple teams’ roadmaps for 2021 and 2022, including:

A brand-new Inbound Performance Dashboard (see the full case study here)
A refreshed Inbound Shipping Queue
New Shipment Details page features
And many more initiatives…

Outcome

The result

The Customer journey map, along with the supporting research report and findings, made up the user-centric rationale for the 3-year Inbound north star Seller Experience.

Many Seller org leaders used the journey map, the research findings, and the accompanying North Star design concepts to further conversations between team members about what the future Seller experience should look like.

As a result, multiple Seller-centric initiatives were added to Amazon’s Operational Planning process and prioritized on multiple teams’ 2021 and 2022 roadmaps, including:

#1 - A brand-new Inbound Performance Dashboard (IPD)

(see the full case study here)

About: The IPD is a one-stop tool allowing Sellers to understand, monitor, and troubleshoot their inbound shipment defects.

Results: The IPD was launched globally in March, 2022 after a successful pilot release (ran Nov-Dec, 2021 and involving 3k Sellers across NA, EU + UK). Pilot Seller’s defect response rate improved 1095 bps (54.85% to 65.80%, beating the target key result of +1000 bps). As a result, pilot Seller’s respective defect rate improved 2129 bps (41.88% to 20.59%, beating the target key result of -500 bps).

#2 - A refreshed Inbound Shipping Queue

About: The new Shipping Queue is aimed at improving Seller understanding in to the overall status of their inbounded shipments.

Results: 16 new Seller-facing improvements discovered through the North star research launched through 2021, as part of a wider initiative to deprecate the existing platform. CX improvements included:

  1. Moved to a new Seller UI design system
  2. Enhanced ingressing to create shipments
  3. Shipment status overview at top of page
  4. Item previews (“what’s in my shipment?”)
  5. Multiple tabs / workflows merged
  6. And many more Seller improvements…

#3 - New Shipment Details page features

About: New Shipment Details page features help to improve Selling Partner Visibility into the details of their shipments.

Results: The team’s 2021 Goal was to improve FBA tracking ID coverage (i.e. the # of units received with correct tracking information provided) by 1500bps YoY for US Shipments. FBA tracking ID coverage for US Small-Parcel (“SPD”) shipments increased 16.67% (beating the 15% goal), while tracking ID coverage for US Less-Than-Truckload (“LTL”) shipments increased 14.55% (just shy of the 15% goal).
