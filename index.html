A US-based, AI-fluent product designer, systems thinker, and creative strategist with expertise in crafting video and live-streaming experiences.

I innovate using human-centered design and a lean, collaborative approach, with AI woven through discovery, prototyping, and delivery, to solve complex problems across products, platforms, and services.

I joined Netflix in 2025, having previously led design at Amazon and Prime Video (2019–2025), R/GA, Razorfish, Beyond, Ogilvy, Publicis Sapient, and Sky.

Away from work, I enjoy the Pacific Northwest's vibrant culture, while staying true to my Northern British roots.
