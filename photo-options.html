Hero photo options

Five ways to show you instead of the Sport Relief image, using your real photos. Click, hover or Tab through each one.

A

One photo

A single taped print, as it is now but with you in it. Calm and simple, with nothing to operate. You choose the one photo that says the most.

hello!

B

Slideshow

One print that cross-fades through all 11 photos, with arrows and a counter. It keeps the controls you already have. Use the arrow buttons or the left and right arrow keys.

01 / 11photos of me

C

Shuffle stack

A pile of snapshots. Click the top one and it slides off to the back, like shuffling prints on a desk. The most tactile, and it suits the paper-and-tape feel.

click me

D

Scrapbook cluster

Four prints overlapping at different angles, each with its own tape. Nothing moves until you hover one, and then it straightens and lifts. Shows more of you at once.

E

Print with filmstrip

One main print with a row of small thumbnails beneath it. Click a thumbnail to swap the main photo. The most browsable of the five, and everything is visible at a glance.
