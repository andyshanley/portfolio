Photo riffs, round two

Four looser fans where the taped top print never moves, and four toss piles with a big stack on the left and smaller prints on the right. Hover or click each one.

Fans

The top print stays stuck

The print with the tape never moves. Hover and the prints behind it spread out, each at its own angle and distance, so it looks scattered rather than arranged. Click to send the top print to the back.

F1 · Loose spread

Hand-placed and lopsided: different angles and distances, nothing symmetrical.

F2 · Pushed sideways

The prints slide out to the right, like a deck pushed across a desk with a thumb.

F3 · Scattered all round

Each print drifts off in its own direction, up, down, left and right.

F4 · Different every time

The spread is re-rolled each time you hover, so it is never the same twice.

Toss piles

A big stack, with smaller prints tossed beside it

The stack on the left is the main event. Click the top print and it is tossed to the right, where it lands smaller. When the stack runs out, everything resets.

T1 · One small pile

Everything you toss lands in a single, tidy-ish heap on the right.

T2 · Messy heap

T3 · Two small piles

Prints alternate between an upper and a lower pile on the right.

T4 · Scattered landing
