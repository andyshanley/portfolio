Demo

Photo riffs

Three riffs on the shuffle stack (C) and three on the scrapbook cluster (D). Hover, click or Tab through each one.

C-1

Fan

The stack sits neatly closed. Hover it and it opens like a hand of cards so you can see the edges of the photos behind. Click to send the top print to the back.

hover, then click

C-2

Toss pile

Click the top print and it is thrown onto a second pile beside the first, so the photos you have seen build up as a heap. When the stack runs out, it resets.

toss it

C-3

Auto-shuffle with a pencil counter

The stack shuffles itself every few seconds through all 11 photos, and pauses when you hover or focus it. A pencil counter in the margin says where you are. You can still click to skip ahead.

1 / 11

D-1

Living cluster

Four prints overlap on the page. Click any one and its photo fades to the next one in the set, so the cluster slowly cycles through all 11 without ever changing shape.

D-2

Lead print with two small ones

One large print leads, with two smaller prints tucked over its corners. A clear hierarchy, so there is a main photo and a bit of supporting texture, still with each print taped on separately.

D-3

Scatter on hover

Five prints piled in a loose heap. When you hover the group they drift gently apart, like you have spread them out on a desk to look through them.
