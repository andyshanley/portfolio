At a glance

The challenge

Through reviewing Seller feedback (through Amazon’s internal HMD/How’s My Driving platform) it was found that the end-to-end Seller inbound journey suffered from a severe lack of interoperability, as the UX was incoherently spread across multiple areas of Amazon’s Seller Central platform. This was leading to an increase in time on task and number of/repetitive steps to task completion for Amazon’s Selling Partners.

The objective

Partner with/encourage FBA Inbound product and tech owners to begin adopting a multi-year, vastly-improved Seller-centric “north star” CX into their respective roadmaps starting 2021.

The solution

Run an in-depth Seller research study, create an end-to-end Customer journey map, an accompanying data-centric research report, and new “north star” UI designs.

The result

Multiple Seller-centric initiatives were added to Amazon’s Operational Planning process and prioritized on multiple teams’ roadmaps for 2021 and 2022, including:

A brand-new Inbound Performance Dashboard (see the full case study here)
A refreshed Inbound Shipping Queue
New Shipment Details page features
And many more initiatives…

At a glance

My role
Design Lead
Partners
Design (x2), Research, Product, Engineering
Timeframe
2 months to develop the framework

The challenge

Even though X-Ray is loved by Customers and recognised as a differentiator among streaming services, the experience had barely evolved. VOD hadn’t been meaningfully updated in over 12 years, and Sports hadn’t received a major update since launch. That led to low discovery and engagement, inconsistency, and a lack of cohesion.

The objective

Create an in-playback framework that solves for these issues, one that is cohesive across all stream types and is extensible to all current and future features.

The solution

On a Living Room remote, Customers can perform one of six actions: Up, Down, Left, Right, Back and Select. Up and Down were our options. The scrubber became the horizon line.

The result

The new framework gained wider adoption across the business, including:

Prioritised across other Live Sports and stream types, including VOD
All future Living Room playback design work uses the framework
34% fewer components than the existing experience on new platforms
An adapted version for mobile
